2. Analytics, visitor telemetry and cookies

Zeriotic measures site performance and visitor journeys to understand how prospective clients, engineering teams and partners discover and explore our work. We use both aggregated measurement and client-side telemetry cookies.

Your consent choices: When you first visit zeriotic.com, we display a cookie banner giving you two direct choices:

  • Accept all cookies: Enables full visitor activity and telemetry cookies, allowing us to measure scroll depth milestones (25% to 100%), chapter dwell times, CTA interactions, and referral attribution.
  • Accept necessary cookies only: Disables all tracking, telemetry and attribution cookies. Only the strictly necessary cookie required to remember your preference is saved.

Cookies and storage keys used on zeriotic.com:

  • zr_consent (Strictly Necessary, 1 year): Remembers whether you chose all cookies or necessary cookies only.
  • zr_prefs (Strictly Necessary, 1 year): Stores granular category choices (necessary, analytics, attribution).
  • _zr_vid (Activity & Telemetry, 1 year): A pseudonymous visitor identifier to distinguish unique visitors across visits.
  • _zr_sid (Activity & Telemetry, 30 minutes rolling): A session identifier refreshed on active browsing.
  • _zr_first_seen & _zr_last_seen (Activity & Telemetry, 1 year): Timestamps of initial discovery and recent interaction.
  • _zr_visit_count (Activity & Telemetry, 1 year): Total number of browsing sessions.
  • _zr_depth (Activity & Telemetry, 30 minutes): Highest scroll depth milestone reached on the page.
  • _zr_act (Activity & Telemetry, 30 minutes): Rolling event stream of recent actions (button clicks, section views).
  • _zr_ref, _zr_landing & _zr_utm (Attribution, 180 days): Initial referring domain, entry landing page, and campaign attribution parameters.

You can change or revoke your cookie choices at any time by clicking in the footer of any page.

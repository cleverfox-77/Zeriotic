Choose Zeriotic when

  • You want the senior people who pitched the work to actually build it.
  • You are shipping something ambitious and want a partner that treats your product like its own.
  • You would rather review working software in staging than a deck in a meeting.
  • You value ownership-grade documentation and a codebase that outlives the engagement.

What Zeriotic does not do

  • We do not subcontract or white-label. The people you meet are the people who build it.
  • We do not staff projects with juniors. Sixteen senior engineers and designers, by design.
  • We do not take unlimited clients. A handful of engagements a quarter, so quality is never spread thin.
  • We do not publish fixed prices or invented "starting at" numbers. Every project is scoped in discovery and quoted as a fixed engagement.
  • We do not fabricate credibility: no invented people, no invented testimonials, no invented metrics.
  • We do not run account-manager relays. You talk to the people doing the work.

Questions, answered

Does Zeriotic outsource or subcontract work?

No. The sixteen-person studio designs and builds every engagement in-house. The people you meet are the people who write the code.

How is a Zeriotic project priced?

As a fixed engagement scoped during discovery. Typical budget bands are $0-$5k, $5k-$10k, $10k-$25k, $25k-$50k, $50k-$100k and $100k+.

Who do I actually work with?

Directly with the senior engineers and designers doing the work - there is no account-manager relay.

How do I see progress?

Through a live staging environment you can click through from week one, so you never rely on a status deck.

How many projects does Zeriotic take on at once?

A handful per year, on purpose. Availability is limited so that senior people stay on your project for its whole life.

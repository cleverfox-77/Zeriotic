Questions, answered

What is OctaDezx?

OctaDezx is an AI customer support agent. It answers customers on chat, messaging apps and the phone, takes orders and booking requests, and hands the hard conversations to your team.

How is OctaDezx different from a chatbot?

A chatbot replies and leaves the work to you. OctaDezx carries the conversation through to an order, a booking request, a clean handover or a follow-up, and checks every written reply against your own records.

Does OctaDezx make things up?

Every written reply is checked against your own records before it is sent. In the published benchmark, 96.7% of deliberately corrupted replies were refused (59 of 61). The test cases are synthetic and the benchmark is not a head-to-head comparison.

Which languages does OctaDezx support?

OctaDezx answers in more than 50 languages and detects the language of each customer automatically.

Plans start at $29 a month, every plan includes every feature, and there is a 3-day free trial with no credit card.

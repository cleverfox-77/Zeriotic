Traditional chatbots frustate buyers with generic scripts, hallucinate non-existent stock or unauthorized discounts, and collapse when customers interrupt them mid-sentence.

Architecture Overview & Deterministic Boundary

The core architectural achievement of the OctaDezx platform is the strict separation between agentic conversational reasoning and deterministic transactional execution. Instead of allowing generative models direct write access to order records, the architecture enforces a hardened validation membrane.

By sandboxing the generative runtime behind cryptographic price-verification gates, OctaDezx guarantees zero pricing hallucinations while maintaining natural, spontaneous conversation flow.

Real-Time Interruptible Voice with Barge-In

Customers rarely speak in linear paragraphs. On telephone calls, people interrupt, clarify, ask for corrections, and speak over explanations. OctaDezx connects directly to real telephone numbers (PSTN and SIP trunks) using a full-duplex, low-latency audio stream.

Audio // 01

Conversational Barge-In

When the caller speaks, the voice pipeline immediately yields within milliseconds, cuts output playback, processes the interruption in context, and adapts its response without robotic pause.

Latency // 02

Sub-300ms Round-Trip Audio

End-to-end edge routing pairs ultra-fast streaming speech recognition with direct audio packet synthesis, making calls feel indistinguishable from speaking with a senior human concierge.

Telephony // 03

Real Phone Numbers & Warm Transfer

Deploys on native regional phone numbers with dual-tone multi-frequency (DTMF) detection, call recording, automated CRM logging, and seamless live transfer to on-duty staff when requested.

Multimodal Vision: Instant Catalog Recognition

In retail, auto parts, furniture, and salon environments, customers often send photos rather than model names. OctaDezx features an instant visual matching pipeline integrated across WhatsApp, Instagram, and web chats.

When a customer uploads an image—whether it is a photo of a dress, an equipment tag, a clinical skin concern, or a room blueprint—the multimodal vision layer extracts spatial, chromatic, and textural vectors. It matches the image against the merchant's live catalog database in sub-second time, pulling the exact SKU, current stock status, and accurate sizing options without requiring text descriptions.

Server-Verified Pricing Integrity

A primary risk of generative sales agents is hallucinated discounts or erroneous checkout totals caused by prompt injection or edge-case confusion. OctaDezx enforces 100% price integrity through mathematical backend verification.

  • Zero Hallucinated Discounts: The AI model cannot alter database pricing, inject custom coupons, or confirm off-book totals. Every line item, tax rate, shipping surcharge, and volume tier is calculated by the merchant's authoritative pricing engine.
  • Cryptographic Quote Tokens: When an order is assembled, the server issues an immutable signed cart payload with an expiration timestamp. The checkout flow completes securely through Stripe, Shopify, or custom merchant gateways.
  • Zero-Leak Sandboxing: System architecture prevents reverse-engineering of internal company guidelines or competitive margins. Adversarial prompt extraction attempts are neutralized at the edge before reaching processing agents.

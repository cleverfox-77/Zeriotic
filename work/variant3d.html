Variant3D - Knitwear, rendered thread by thread

Domain3D Fashion Tech
StackWebGL · Three.js
RegionUnited States

The Problem

Variant3D needed a way to showcase their on-demand knitwear platform without photography - garments that don't exist yet can't be shot.

The Approach

Built a real-time 3D WebGL showcase where every garment renders as living geometry - stitch patterns, material physics, and color variations orbit-able in the browser.

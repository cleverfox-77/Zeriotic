Client project | Knitwear

Variant3D. Every garment, up close.

An interactive showcase for an on-demand knitwear platform. Visitors turn every garment and look closely before they buy.

The brief

Variant3D runs an on-demand knitwear platform. That creates an unusual problem for selling it: a garment that has not been made yet cannot be photographed. Buyers still want to know what they are getting, and a flat picture was never going to answer that. They needed to see the knit, the colour and the shape for themselves before deciding.

What we made

We made an interactive showcase that puts each garment in the visitor's hands. Every piece can be turned and looked at closely, the way you would pick up a jumper in a shop and turn it over. Nothing stands between the visitor and the product, so the decision to buy comes from having really looked at it.

Buyers explore before they commit, which is exactly what a made-to-order platform needs from its website.

The Vision

Mobile racing titles historically make severe compromises: physics calculations get simplified to unphysical arcade heuristics, frame updates suffer from non-deterministic drift, and wheel contact mechanics are averaged into crude collision boxes.

Zeriotic Labs engineered Accert One to overturn these constraints. The objective was clear: architect a true motorsport simulator with deterministic lockstep physics, sub-millimeter raycast tire deformation, and active aerodynamic modeling, delivering 60 FPS performance on handheld devices without sacrificing simulation purity.

Deterministic Physics Engine

At the center of Accert One is a custom, mathematical physics solver built in C# to eliminate floating-point non-determinism across disparate CPU architectures. Every calculation executes in a fixed 16.66ms tick rate, guaranteeing identical simulation trajectories across Android and iOS hardware.

01 // Dynamics

Multi-Link Suspension Kinematics

Independent multi-link geometry calculates anti-roll bar torsion, dynamic camber gain under chassis roll, spring compression curves, and high-frequency damper rebound per wheel.

02 // Contact

Raycast Tire Contact Model

Continuous downward and lateral raycasts sample track surface normals at millimeter resolution. Computes combined slip angles, friction ellipses, and tire carcass deflection dynamically.

03 // Aero

Active DRS & Ground-Effect Aero

Computes front splitter downforce, floor venturi tunnel ground effects, and rear wing drag coefficients. Features active DRS actuation with instantaneous flap balance adjustments.

04 // Sync

Ghost & Telemetry Synchronization

Stores high-density lap packets capturing throttle trace, steering angle, brake pressure, and wheel velocity. Enables frame-perfect ghost playback and zero-desync competitive multiplayer.

Dynamic Atmospheric Simulation

Weather is not a cosmetic visual layer in Accert One; it directly reshapes the mathematical friction coefficient of every square centimeter of track surface.

  • Dry Track: Dynamic rubber deposit builds along the racing line as laps accumulate, raising optimal tire grip by up to 14% while marbles form off-line.
  • Rain Conditions: Simulates dynamic water film thickness, puddle accumulation in low-camber depressions, progressive aquaplaning danger, and altered tire cooling.
  • Snow Simulation: Sub-zero track surface temperatures alter compound viscoelasticity, demanding delicate throttle inputs and adapted braking distance vectors.
  • Night Racing: Dynamic HDR illumination featuring volumetric projector beam lighting, trackside floodlight shadows, and real-time brake disc thermal glow.

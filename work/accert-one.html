Accert One. Every lap is earned.

Accert One is single-seater racing, built for your phone. Line up on the grid, read the sky, and race a full field from lights out to the chequered flag. Tight street circuits punish one mistake. Fast open country rewards nerve. Day, sunset, night, rain and snow all change how the car feels under you.

Call your pit stop. Manage your tyres. Keep it clean, because race control is watching. Win a round, then go after the championship.

Questions, answered

What is Accert One?

Accert One is a single-seater mobile racing game from Zeriotic. You race a full field through day, night, rain and snow, call your pit stops and chase a championship.

What platforms will Accert One be on?

Accert One is coming to iOS and Android.

Is Accert One free?

Yes. Accert One will be free to download.

When does Accert One come out?

Accert One is in development and does not have a public release date yet. Pre-order and we will tell you the moment it is ready.

How do I pre-order Accert One?

Enter your email in the pre-order box on this page. We only use it for launch news.

Is Accert One connected to a real racing series?

No. Accert One is an original game with its own cars, teams and drivers. It is not licensed by or connected to any real racing series.

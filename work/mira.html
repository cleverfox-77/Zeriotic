Mira INC. A site that shows, not tells.

An interactive product site for an AI company, where the visuals show what the product does.

The brief

Mira INC makes an AI product that was too abstract for a conventional marketing site. Screenshots could not convey what it does, and a page of feature claims would only have asked visitors to take the company's word for it. The site had to make the product understood, not just described.

What we made

We made an interactive product site built around what the product actually does. As visitors move down the page, the visuals demonstrate the idea in front of them, so reading about the product and seeing it at work become the same experience. The copy stays short because the visuals carry the argument.

The site is the first demonstration of the product, and every visitor sees it before they ever ask for one.

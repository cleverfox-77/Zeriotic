Melem Tokyo. Made for the drop.

A global storefront for a Tokyo streetwear label, made for limited drops.

The brief

Melem Tokyo is a streetwear label with an editorial point of view and customers well beyond Japan. Its releases are limited, so the moment a drop goes live is the moment that counts. The label needed a storefront that looked as considered as the clothes and still made buying simple, because most fashion sites end up giving up one for the other.

What we made

We made a global storefront where the art direction and the checkout belong to the same experience. Layouts are quiet, the typography is exact, and the path from product to purchase never breaks the mood. Each limited release gets room to be the event it is, and a customer anywhere in the world can go from first look to finished order without losing the thread.

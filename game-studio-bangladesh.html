Pioneering original game development in Bangladesh

The regional gaming sector in Bangladesh has traditionally been limited to hyper-casual mobile reskins or outsourcing 3D asset modeling for overseas studios. Zeriotic was founded to change this narrative by developing world-class, physics-driven original titles and complex interactive simulations directly from Dhaka.

Our gaming division combines mathematical physics models, real-time lighting pipelines, and responsive touch controls. With Accert One, our upcoming flagship mobile racing game, we are bringing authentic single-seater motorsport simulation to smartphones: full grids of competing AI vehicles, dynamic rain and day-night lighting transitions, tire wear thermodynamics, and live pit wall strategy.

In addition to mobile games, our studio builds cutting-edge WebGL experiences and real-time product configurators that bridge video game rendering techniques with modern web architecture.

Technical capabilities and game engineering

Physics Engine and Vehicle Dynamics

Custom wheel slip algorithms, downforce aero modeling, suspension deflection, and tire wear curves that simulate the tactile feel of driving a high-horsepower single-seater racing car at over 300 km/h.

Atmospheric and Environmental Rendering

Real-time dynamic weather systems featuring transitional rain, spray clouds from tires, evolving wet track grip lines, night track illumination, and procedural skyboxes optimized for mobile GPUs.

Competitive Opponent AI

Spatial pathfinding and overtaking heuristics that deliver intense wheel-to-wheel racing. AI opponents defend their racing line, make pit stop decisions based on tire condition, and respond dynamically to player mistakes.

WebGL and 3D Interactive Web Showrooms

Bringing console-level 3D interactivity to standard web browsers using Three.js, glTF compression, and on-demand render policies that leave the visitor's GPU cool during reading.

Frequently asked questions

What makes Zeriotic the top game studio in Bangladesh?

Unlike casual mobile clone studios, Zeriotic engineers original 3D simulation titles from the ground up in Dhaka. Our flagship title, Accert One, features sophisticated tire-wear physics, dynamic weather cycles, multi-car AI grids, and custom mobile rendering pipelines for iOS and Android.

What platforms does Zeriotic develop games for?

Zeriotic develops games primarily for iOS and Android smartphones, alongside cross-platform WebGL 3D browser simulations that execute at smooth 60 frames per second without requiring plugin downloads.

What is Accert One and when is it releasing?

Accert One is an original single-seater formula-style mobile racing game developed by Zeriotic. It features full-field grand prix racing, tire strategy, rain and snow transitions, and a single-player championship mode. It is currently in active development with pre-orders open at zeriotic.com.

Does Zeriotic accept custom game development and 3D simulation contracts?

Yes. Zeriotic partners with brands, educational institutions, and product companies to engineer interactive 3D simulations, gamified training environments, and interactive WebGL showrooms.

How does Zeriotic ensure high frame rates on budget mobile hardware?

We implement custom shader passes, aggressive draw call batching, LOD (Level of Detail) meshes, and closed-loop dynamic resolution scaling to maintain steady 60 FPS performance across diverse mobile GPUs.

(03) · How we work

Four phases. No ceremony, no surprises.

  1. Phase 01

    Discover & define

    We interrogate the problem. Stakeholder interviews, an audit of existing systems, competitive mapping — and a clear success metric before a single pixel is drawn.

    1-2 wks
  2. Phase 02

    Design the system

    Product architecture, user flows, a full design system and prototype. Every decision pressure-tested against the success metric from phase one.

    2-4 wks
  3. Phase 03

    Engineer & ship

    Senior engineers only. We build in weekly slices against a staging environment you can click through from day one. No black-box development.

    4-10 wks
  4. Phase 04

    Iterate in the open

    Launch is the midpoint, not the finish line. We instrument, measure, iterate — and hand over ownership-grade documentation so the product outlives the engagement.

    Ongoing

Questions, answered

What does Zeriotic build?

High-performance web applications, e-commerce platforms and SaaS products, plus AI integration and interactive 3D/WebGL product experiences.

How much does a Zeriotic project cost?

Projects are scoped during discovery. Typical budget bands are $10k to $25k, $25k to $50k, $50k to $100k and $100k+.

How does the engagement process work?

Four phases: discover and define (1-2 weeks), design the system (2-4 weeks), engineer and ship (4-10 weeks), then iterate in the open. You click through a staging environment from day one.

Where is Zeriotic based and who does it work with?

Zeriotic is headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh and serves clients worldwide. It is an independent team of sixteen, founded in 2022.

When can Zeriotic start a new project?

Zeriotic takes on a handful of engagements each quarter. Email hello@zeriotic.com and you will get a response within 24 hours.

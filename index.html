Questions, answered

What does Zeriotic build?

Almost every type of software, including web and mobile applications, e-commerce platforms, SaaS and AI products, games, business systems and security testing. Zeriotic also makes its own titles: the OctaDezx AI support agent and the Accert One racing game.

What is OctaDezx?

OctaDezx is an AI customer support agent from Zeriotic. It answers customers on chat, messaging apps and the phone, takes orders and booking requests, and checks every written reply against the business's own records before a customer sees it.

How accurate is OctaDezx?

In its published safety benchmark, run on 2026-11-03, 86.8% of 303 multi-step test cases ended in a safe outcome, with 0 privacy leaks in 24 targeted tests. The cases are synthetic and the benchmark is not a head-to-head comparison.

What is Accert One?

Accert One is a single-seater mobile racing game from Zeriotic, coming to iOS and Android. Players race a full field through day, night, rain and snow, call their pit stops and chase a championship.

How do I pre-order Accert One?

Enter your email in the pre-order box on zeriotic.com. The game is free to download, and you will be told the moment it is ready on iOS and Android.

Does Zeriotic offer penetration testing?

Yes. Zeriotic runs independent security testing for web platforms, APIs and AI products, including penetration testing, application security review, and AI and agent security, followed by help with the fix and a retest.

How much does a Zeriotic project cost?

Projects are scoped during discovery. Typical budget bands are $0 to $5k, $5k to $10k, $10k to $25k, $25k to $50k, $50k to $100k and $100k+.

How does the engagement process work?

Four phases: discover and define (1-2 weeks), design the system (2-4 weeks), engineer and ship (4-10 weeks), then iterate in the open. You click through a staging environment from day one.

Where is Zeriotic based and who does it work with?

Zeriotic is headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh and serves clients worldwide. It was founded in 2022 and works with businesses of any size.

When can Zeriotic start a new project?

Zeriotic is taking on new projects of almost every type. Email hello@zeriotic.com and you will get a response within 24 hours.
